📐 Forecast & Math Engine (Visual Explanation & Governors)

Clear plain-English explanation of how your survival numbers are calculated with zero magic.

📖 How the Deterministic Math Works (Step-by-Step)

You never have to guess where your money went. The engine calculates everything deterministically in two simple steps:

STEP 1: Current Month Spendable Cash
Spendable Balance = Total Salary (Rs. 399,585) - Total Committed Bills (Rs. 251,745) - Daily Spends

Committed Bills includes your Rent, ECB+Water, Bank Loans, Koko installments, CC dues, and Subscriptions. Deducting them upfront ensures you never accidentally spend rent money!

STEP 2: Next Month Forward Survival Forecast
Next Month Surplus = Next Month Salary - (Recurring Fixed Bills + Continuing BNPL + Subscriptions)

BNPL plans that finish this cycle (e.g. 3/3 installments paid) are automatically excluded from next month's committed costs, freeing up your cashflow!

⚙️ Tunable Engine Governors (Parameters)

Adjust how conservative or aggressive the app should be in protecting your household.

What is this? A safety cushion percentage (default 5% = approx Rs. 19,979) locked away in case next month's salary is delayed or unexpected expenses arise.

Frequently Asked Questions & How-To Guides

📅 Why does our budget run from 25th to 25th?

In Sri Lanka, major corporate salaries and bank payments are credited on the 25th. Running budgets on calendar months (1st to 30th) causes misleading balances because rent and loan deductions happen on the 25th. This app guarantees your spendable cash matches your actual salary rhythm.

🛍️ How do Koko and Mintpay BNPL plans affect our budget?

When you purchase an item via Koko (e.g. Rs. 15,000 in 3 installments of Rs. 5,000), only Rs. 5,000 is deducted in the current cycle. In the 3rd and final month, the engine automatically frees up that Rs. 5,000 for next month's forward survival forecast.

✨ Should I buy items on my Wishlist this month?

Look at the Projected Savings at Cycle End on the top banner. If projected savings is greater than the wishlist item cost, and Next Month is in Surplus, you can safely purchase it without going into credit card debt!

🎨 How do I change colors and branding with zero redeployment?

Navigate to Appearance / Themes in the CMS sidebar. Click any theme palette or write custom CSS in the live editor and click Save & Publish. All changes apply immediately across all client browsers without reloading code.

⚙️ How Sync Works

Hybrid Strategy (fast + durable):

1. App start: Load from localStorage instantly (zero delay). Then fetch cloud state in background.
2. Conflict resolution: Whichever has a newer timestamp wins. If cloud is newer → apply + re-render with toast "☁️ Synced from cloud".
3. Every save: persistState() writes to localStorage immediately, then debounces a cloud push 800ms later (so rapid typing/editing doesn't flood the API).
4. Offline: If Supabase is unreachable, the sync indicator shows 📴 Offline. All your data is safely in localStorage and will sync next time you're online.

Data table: household_state — 1 row per household, full state stored as JSONB.

📸 Cloud Snapshots & State History

Manually save named snapshots at any time and restore them — like WordPress Revisions.

Every snapshot stores the entire app state (all members, incomes, bills, BNPL, spends, settings, labels).
Restoring a snapshot replaces the current state and syncs to cloud immediately.
Snapshots are stored in the household_state_history table (unlimited retention on Supabase free tier).
