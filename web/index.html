📅 Why 25th-to-25th Cycle Sync Matters

In corporate environments, salaries are credited on the 25th, but rent and loan deductions happen immediately. Traditional calendar month trackers (1st–30th) give a false illusion of excess money in early days, leading to mid-month cashflow crises.

HomeBudget synchronizes directly with your salary payday, reserving all mandatory bills first and giving you a 100% mathematically accurate spendable daily budget.
